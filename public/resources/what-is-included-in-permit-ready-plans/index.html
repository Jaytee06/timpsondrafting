What Is Included in Permit-Ready Plans?

A practical guide to defining a residential permit drawing package and confirming local requirements.

By Timpson Drafting & Design · Published July 20, 2026 · Updated July 20, 2026

“Permit-ready” describes a defined package, not guaranteed approval

A residential permit package commonly combines a cover sheet, site information, floor plans, exterior elevations, building sections, and project-specific details. The exact sheets depend on the jurisdiction, occupancy, project scope, and supporting consultants.

Confirm these items before drafting

  • The authority having jurisdiction and its current checklist
  • Property survey, setbacks, utility, and site information
  • Existing-condition documentation for additions or remodels
  • Energy, structural, civil, geotechnical, or other consultant requirements
  • File format, submission method, and responsible applicant

Timpson defines the proposed sheet list in the quote. Review comments may still require revisions, and permit approval remains with the reviewing authority.
