Draftsman vs. Architect vs. Engineer

Understand how residential drafting, architecture, and engineering roles differ before choosing a project team.

By Timpson Drafting & Design · Published July 20, 2026 · Updated July 20, 2026

Choose by responsibility, not job title

A residential drafter documents an agreed design and construction scope. An architect provides architectural services where design responsibility or licensure is required. An engineer evaluates and designs structural or other technical systems within the engineer’s discipline. One project may need more than one of these roles.

A practical decision framework

  1. Ask the building department whether stamped architectural or engineering documents are required.
  2. Identify structural changes, unusual site conditions, and work outside a drafting scope.
  3. Confirm who owns design decisions and consultant coordination.
  4. Get each provider’s deliverables and exclusions in writing.

Timpson starts by reviewing the location, project type, source documents, and intended use of the drawings. If the known scope indicates outside professional work, that dependency should be identified before the drawing package is promised.
