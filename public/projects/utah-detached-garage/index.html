Utah Detached Garage

A compact detached-garage drawing set showing coordinated exterior elevations and a building section.

Project challenge

Document a straightforward accessory structure with enough exterior and sectional information to communicate its form and construction intent.

Drafting approach

TDD assembled multiple exterior elevations with a cross section and focused wall detail on a single coordinated sheet.

Deliverables

  • Exterior elevations
  • Building cross section
  • Wall and foundation detail

Result

The sheet gives the project team a compact visual reference for the garage exterior and building assembly.

Measurement context: The 720 sq ft figure is the detached-garage area stated in the source set, not living or heated area.
